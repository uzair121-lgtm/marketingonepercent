# 1% app — canary user acquisition

**Objective:** 100 canary users by **30 Sep 2026**.

A **canary user** = installed the app **and** completed first-run setup. An install that never opens does not count. *Activation event pending founder confirmation — see `plan/measurement.md`.*

## Targets

| Source | Target | Working deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paid, IG only | 60 | 14 Sep |
| Free channels | 40 | 14 Sep |
| Buffer (recovery only) | 16 days | 30 Sep |

The 16 days to month end are recovery time for one channel underdelivering, **not** planned capacity. Do not spend the buffer in advance.

## Hard constraints

- Budget: **$20/day** paid. No increase without founder approval.
- CPI: **$7 ceiling** (campaign average), **$5 target** (best variant).
- Paid channel: **Instagram only**. No Facebook placements, no Google, no TikTok.
- Creative: **no faces**. Product shots, screen recordings, text treatments only.
- Founder time is the scarce input — a few hours a week.

## Paid math (validates the 60)

- $20/day × 21 days = **$420** spend.
- At $7 CPI ceiling → **60 installs**. At $5 best-variant → 84. 21 days of spend is the minimum to land 60 at the ceiling, so any launch slip past **24 Aug** costs installs one-for-one.
