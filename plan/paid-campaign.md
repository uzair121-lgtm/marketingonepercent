# Paid campaign (Instagram only)

**Launch 24 Aug. Target 60 canary installs by 14 Sep.**

## Setup
- Objective: app installs
- Placement: **Instagram only** — no Facebook, Google, TikTok
- Targeting: broad, age 25–45, fitness interests
- Budget: $20/day, fixed. No increase without founder approval.
- Formats: 4:5 and 9:16
- Creative rule: **no faces** — product shots, screen recordings, text treatments only

## Six variants (ready 18 Aug)
Log each in `tracking/paid-variants.csv`. Weekly review: cut any variant above **$7 CPI**, shift budget to the survivor.
