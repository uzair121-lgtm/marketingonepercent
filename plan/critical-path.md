## Escalate immediately (not at the weekly) if

- Ad account held past **5 Aug**
- Attribution test fails after **14 Aug**
- Listing rejected **twice**
- Blended CPI above **$7 for three consecutive days**

## Known risks

1. **100 followers in 3 weeks is the softest number.** No-faces account, weekly product posts, slow organic reach. If followers stall below 40 by 17 Aug, free-channel outreach absorbs the gap.
2. **Ad account approval sits with Meta** — accounts + payment in on 30 Jul to start the clock early.
3. **A store rejection costs 3–4 days.** One budgeted 17–21 Aug; a second eats the September buffer.
