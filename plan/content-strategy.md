# IG organic content strategy (no faces)

Goal: hit weekly follower floors (`tracking/ig-followers.csv`), feed bio-link installs and named leads. Constraint: no faces — product shots, screen recordings, text treatments only.

## The mechanism
Reach ≠ followers. **Reels are the follower engine; saves and shares trigger reach; a series earns the follow.** Likes don't distribute — ignore them. Every post must (a) earn a save or share and (b) imply "there's more of this here." The "1% compounding" premise is inherently a series — use it.

## Four pillars
1. **Product-in-motion** (screen recordings) — the satisfying core-loop moment; first frame = payoff. Highest install-intent.
2. **Marginal-gains education** (carousels + text) — the 1% philosophy made concrete. Highest save rate.
3. **Proof-through-data** (screens/graphs, never physiques) — streaks, progress lines, "30 days of 1%".
4. **Text-hook Reels** (POV/relatable over B-roll or screen) — cheapest to make, highest share rate when the line lands.

## First-1-second rule
80% of outcome = opening frame/line. Every post opens on a curiosity or outcome gap. One idea per post. Caption ends with a series-based follow reason ("Day 3 of the 1% build — follow along").

## Cadence
- Floor: 3×/week (to get 9 posts before ads).
- **Recommended: daily Reels through 23 Aug** (followers is the softest number; Reels reward volume), then 4–5×/week once ads carry reach.
- **Batch** to protect founder time: one 2-hr session = 6–8 screen clips + 6–8 text hooks, then post from a queue.

## Non-posting levers
- Reply to every comment in the first hour (comment velocity extends reach). Log access requests as named leads.
- Trending audio on every Reel.
- Daily Stories to retain new followers (polls, "1% today").
- Collab posts with fitness micro-accounts (their faces, shared to both audiences) — one good collab > a solo week.
- Consistent visual identity (one font, one color, one layout) so a stranger recognizes post #3.

## Measure / kill
Track saves + shares + follows-per-post in `tracking/ig-posts.csv`, not likes. After ~9 posts, double down on the converting pillar, drop the weakest. If saves/post aren't climbing by ~10 Aug and followers track under the 40 floor, organic won't carry it → shift weight to free channels.

## Hook bank (adapt per post)
- "Doing this 1% thing for 30 days did *this* →"
- "You don't need discipline. You need this."
- "The math that made me stop quitting." (carousel: 1% daily = 37× / year)
- "Save this before your next workout."
- "POV: you stopped chasing 100% and did 1% instead."
- "Everyone quits at week 2. Here's the fix." 
- "What 1% better actually looks like on day 1 vs day 30." (screen recording)
