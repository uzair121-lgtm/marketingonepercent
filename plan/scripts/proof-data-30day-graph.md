# Reel script — "30 days of 1%. Here's the graph."

**Pillar:** proof-through-data · **Format:** screen recording + data reveal, no faces · **Length:** ~25s · **Bank id:** 9
**Goal:** save (people save proof to act on later) + follow (they want to see day 60)

## Pre-production
- Source data: a real 30-day progress state in the app. **If 30 days of real data doesn't exist yet (pre-launch), use the founder's own earliest streak or a representative seeded state — and don't fake a number you can't stand behind.** The graph shape matters more than the exact figures.
- Screen-record at highest res, portrait 9:16. Clean status bar (full battery, no notifications).
- Trending audio: pick a low-key building/lo-fi track (audio does the emotional lift since there's no VO/face).

## Shot list

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0–1.5s | Hard cut straight to the finished graph, then it snaps back to empty | **"30 days of 1%."** | Open on the payoff for half a beat, then reset to day 0 — creates the "wait, how?" gap |
| 1.5–4s | App on day 1: flat line, streak = 1, one small action logged | "Day 1. One 1% action." | Slow, mundane on purpose |
| 4–9s | Fast time-lapse scrub: streak counter ticking 1→30, line climbing | "1% better. Every day." | Screen recording sped up; let the number climb visibly |
| 9–16s | Land on day 30: full graph, streak 30, the compounding curve | **"37% better in a month."** | The math beat — 1.01^30 ≈ 1.35, round honestly to ~35–37% |
| 16–21s | Slow pan across the curve; highlight the bend where it accelerates | "The gains hide in the boring days." | The insight = the save trigger |
| 21–25s | App home screen, subtle CTA card | **"Day 60 loads next. Follow."** | Series promise = the follow trigger |

## Caption
> 30 days of doing just 1% more. Not 100%. Not a full overhaul. 1%.
> The first two weeks look like nothing. Then the curve bends.
> Save this for the day you want to quit. Following the build to day 60 → [link in bio]

## CTA stack
- In-video: "Save this" (text, ~0.5s at the 16s mark) + "Follow" at end
- Caption: save + follow + link in bio
- First pinned comment: repeat the 1% math in one line (comment velocity)

## Why this should convert
- Data reveal = inherently save-worthy (proof you return to).
- The "boring days" insight gives a share reason ("send this to someone quitting").
- "Day 60 next" is an explicit series promise — the follow trigger for a faceless account.
