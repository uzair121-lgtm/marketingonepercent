## Repo orientation (facts only, not process)

`marketingonepercent` holds go-to-market work. The 1% **app** is a separate
codebase and is not in this repository.

- `plan/` — content strategy, organic and paid channels, critical path,
  measurement
- `creative/` — video assets and the Python generator that builds them
- `tracking/` — CSVs for leads, IG posts and followers, canary users, paid
  variants
- `reports/` — weekly reports and the template
